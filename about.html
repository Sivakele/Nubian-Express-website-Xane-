Mission & Vision - Nubian Express

About Nubian Express

Nubian Express was founded by Xitlhangu Khoza, a young entrepreneur determined to bring quality, reliability, and opportunity to South Africa's uniform manufacturing industry.

Mission

To provide businesses, organisations, schools and teams with high quality uniforms and professional branding solutions that strengthen their identity, improve their presentation and help them stand out.

We are committed to delivering quality products, reliable service, creative solutions and professional customer experiences. All of this while building long lasting relationships with every client we serve.

We do not just brand clothing. We help businesses build an identity they can be proud of.

Vision

To become one of South Africa's most trusted and recognised uniform and branding solutions companies. Nubian Express will be known for quality, innovation, reliability and exceptional service.

We envision a Nubian Express that grows from a small local business into a powerful national brand, creating opportunities for employees, entrepreneurs and communities while helping thousands of businesses and organisations present themselves with confidence.

From one uniform to an entire brand, Nubian Express will be the service provider to trust.

Our Values

  • Quality — Every uniform is made with care and attention to detail.
  • Reliability — We deliver on our promises, on time.
  • Integrity — Honest, fair service to every client.
  • Community — Creating opportunities and jobs locally.
