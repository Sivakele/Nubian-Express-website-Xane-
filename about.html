About Nubian Express

Nubian Express was founded by Xitlhangu Khoza, a young entrepreneur determined to address South Africa's persistent unemployment crisis by building a uniform manufacturer that could compete on quality while creating real opportunity.

Our Story

Before starting the company, Xitlhangu worked in a sweatshop-style manufacturing environment — an experience that gave him first-hand insight into the uniform manufacturing industry, its inefficiencies, and the gap in the market for a manufacturer that combines quality, reliability, and fair practice.

Drawing on that experience, he identified an opportunity to build a business that could both compete on quality and create meaningful employment in local communities. That vision became Nubian Express — a company built to manufacture high-quality uniforms for schools and corporations while creating jobs and opportunity where it operates.

Mission

To provide businesses, organisations, schools and teams with high quality uniforms and professional branding solutions that strengthen their identity, improve their presentation and help them stand out.

We are committed to delivering quality products, reliable service, creative solutions and professional customer experiences, all while building long-lasting relationships with every client we serve. We do not just brand clothing — we help businesses build an identity they can be proud of.

Vision

To become one of South Africa's most trusted and recognised uniform and branding solutions companies. Nubian Express will be known for quality, innovation, reliability and exceptional service.

We envision Nubian Express growing from a small local business into a powerful national brand, creating opportunities for employees, entrepreneurs and communities, while helping thousands of businesses present themselves with confidence.

Our Values

  • Quality — Every uniform is made with care and attention to detail.
  • Reliability — We deliver on our promises, on time.
  • Integrity — Honest, fair service to every client.
  • Community — Creating opportunities and jobs locally.

From one uniform to an entire brand, Nubian Express will be the service provider to trust.
